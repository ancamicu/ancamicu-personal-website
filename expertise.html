Expertise focus

AACSB accreditation

CIR preparation. Continuous improvement reviews are consultative rather than standard-by-standard, with the six-year cycle centered on continuous improvement, strategic management, and mission fulfillment.
Faculty qualifications frameworks. The 2026 standards classify faculty as SA, PA, SP, IP, or Additional, and tie maintenance of status to role-appropriate activity plus teaching effectiveness.
Coverage analysis and scenario planning. Schools are normally expected to maintain participating faculty teaching above 75 percent overall and above 60 percent by discipline, with 40 percent SA and 90 percent SA+PA+SP+IP expectations used in Table 3-1 analysis.
Risk scenario planning. Strategic plans are expected to include formal risk analysis and mitigation for mission-critical threats, from staffing and financial issues to technological disruption and cybersecurity.
Societal impact and assessment processes. Practical guidance on linking strategy, AoL, scholarship, and societal impact evidence into a coherent CIR narrative.

AI strategy for business schools

AI-supported reporting workflows. Build more efficient processes for drafting narratives, organizing evidence, summarizing standards, and structuring accreditation documentation responsibly.
Leadership guidance. Translate AI from an abstract priority into governance, faculty support, academic innovation, and clearer institutional decision-making.
Implementation support. Help schools move from “interesting AI ideas” to usable systems, templates, and internal workflows.
Mission-fit innovation. Use AI in ways that support quality, not just speed—especially in curriculum, faculty development, continuous improvement, and student-facing operations.

CIR toolkit

Standards snapshot

The newly published 2026 AACSB Global Standards position business education around nine standards across strategic management, learner success, and pathways to impact, while emphasizing global standards with local application.

Visit preparation

The CIR handbook emphasizes that the review is not a standard-by-standard audit. Instead, it is a consultative process focused on mission, strategy, educational quality, and continuous improvement, with the final CIR report distributed 60 days before the visit.

Documentation strategy

The CIR report guidelines call for a concise, forward-looking narrative supported by core uploads such as the strategic plan, risk analysis, faculty criteria, and AACSB tables including 2-1, 3-1, 3-2, and 8-1.

Interactive tools

These practical tools are designed for planning conversations, scenario testing, and internal analysis.

Undergraduate coverage calculator

Enter total undergraduate sections, discipline mix, full-time faculty counts by qualification, and normal section loads. Remaining uncovered sections are treated as IP adjuncts teaching one section each.

Discipline percentages must sum to exactly 100. The calculator checks for over-assigned full-time teaching.

Graduate coverage calculator

For the target graduate program, enter the number of sections offered and the number of faculty in each qualification category plus the average number of sections each teaches in that program.

If faculty coverage does not reach the total, the remaining sections are assumed to be taught by IP adjuncts at one section per year.

Faculty qualifications estimator

Use this as a planning aid to estimate likely classification under a school’s framework aligned with AACSB categories.
