About

Short bio

Anca C. Micu, PhD is a marketing professor and senior business school leader whose work spans advertising, digital communication, AACSB accreditation, and AI-enabled innovation in higher education. She began her career in Romania’s first major advertising agency, developed scholarly expertise in online advertising and communication strategy, and later built a leadership portfolio that connects academic quality, institutional positioning, faculty collaboration, and practical innovation.

Professional profile

Her background is unusually integrative. She has moved comfortably across agency work, doctoral research, consulting-style analytical roles, teaching, and academic administration. That breadth helps her bring both narrative clarity and operational realism to complex institutional challenges.

In addition to her leadership work, she has served as an accreditation-focused advisor, conference presenter, author, and thought leader on how business schools can respond to rapid technological change while maintaining educational quality and human judgment.
